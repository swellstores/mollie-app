Give your shoppers the payment methods they actually use. With Mollie, customers in the Netherlands pay with iDEAL, in Belgium with Bancontact, and everywhere with cards, PayPal, Apple Pay and bank transfer, all through one Mollie account.

## One integration, every method

Add Mollie to your Swell checkout in a few minutes. Shoppers choose Mollie, pay on Mollie's secure payment page with the method they prefer, and come straight back to a confirmed order. You decide which methods to offer in your Mollie dashboard; new methods appear at checkout without touching your store.

## Built for European shoppers

- **iDEAL and Bancontact** for the Dutch and Belgian shoppers who expect them
- **Cards, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay** for everyone else
- **Pay later with Klarna, in3 and Riverty**, where your Mollie account offers them: the app sends Mollie the order details these methods need

## Safe by design

- Every payment is checked with Mollie before an order is marked paid, and the amount must match the order exactly.
- Card details and bank logins never touch your store: shoppers pay on Mollie's page.
- If a shopper pays but closes the page before returning, the app creates their order automatically. Anything it can't sort out lands in a **Needs attention** list, and you get an email.

## Everything from your Swell dashboard

- **Refund** in full or in part from the order page.
- **Mollie payments** under Orders shows every payment and what happened to it.
- A **status panel** shows that your Mollie connection works and which methods are turned on.

Test with Mollie's test mode first, then switch to your live key when you're ready.
