First release of Mollie for Swell.

- **Pay with Mollie at checkout:** shoppers choose Mollie and pay on Mollie's secure payment page with iDEAL, Bancontact, cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, bank transfer and every other method your Mollie account offers.
- **Payments checked with Mollie:** an order is marked paid only when Mollie confirms the payment and the amount matches the order total.
- **Refunds from Swell:** refund in full or in part from the order page.
- **Pay-later ready:** order lines and addresses are sent to Mollie for Klarna, in3, Riverty and Billie.
- **Safety net:** if a shopper pays and closes the page before returning, the app creates their order. Payments it can't match are listed under Orders > Mollie payments > Needs attention, and store admins get an email.
- **Status panel:** Apps > Mollie > Settings shows whether your Mollie connection works and which payment methods are turned on.

Setup guide: https://developers.swell.is/app-guides/mollie
